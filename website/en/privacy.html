Privacy policy

Custos Android app. Last updated: October 1, 2026.

In short

  • Custos does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
  • The app does not request the internet permission: it cannot send any data off your phone.
  • No account, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDK.
  • What Custos stores stays in the app's private storage on your phone, and is removed when you uninstall it.

2. No data collected

Custos runs entirely on your phone. It has no server, no user account, no analytics and no ads. The app manifest does not declare the android.permission.INTERNET permission, so Android does not let it send data over the network. You can check it in the AndroidManifest.xml file.

The publisher of Custos therefore receives and holds no data about you.

3. What is stored on your phone

To enforce your rules, Custos stores the following locally, in the app's private storage:

  • your rules: limited apps, quotas, curfews, allowed days and hours, session limits, Nuclear Mode settings and presets, protection delays;
  • your partial access rules: the app concerned and technical identifiers of screen elements, used to recognise the section to block (not the content on screen);
  • today's state: usage time of the limited apps, sessions used, apps currently blocked;
  • your preferences, for example pausing the notifications of a blocked app.

Custos does not record a history of the apps you open. To erase everything, uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings (Apps, Custos, Storage).

Custos does not turn off Android's automatic backup. If it is enabled on your phone, Android may include the Custos settings in your Google account backup, as it does for other apps. That backup is handled by Android and Google, not by Custos.

4. Permissions requested and why

PermissionUse
Accessibility service Know which app is in the foreground, send you back to the home screen when a rule applies, protect sensitive settings pages and recognise the screens targeted by a partial access rule. What is on screen is analysed on the spot, is not stored and is never transmitted.
Usage access Measure the time spent in each limited app.
List of installed apps Show you your apps when you choose which ones to limit.
Notifications Show the monitoring service notification (Android 13 and up).
Notification access (optional) Pause the notifications of blocked apps, then restore them. Their content is not stored.
Do Not Disturb (optional) Turn on Do Not Disturb during Nuclear Mode.
Start at boot, exact alarms, foreground service Restart the protection when the phone boots or if the service is stopped.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time in Android settings. Some Custos features then stop working.

5. Sharing with third parties

None. Custos contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting SDK, and shares no data with anyone.
